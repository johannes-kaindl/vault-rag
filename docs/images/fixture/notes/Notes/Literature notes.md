# Literature notes

A literature note records what a source said, in your own words, with a pointer back to where you found it. It is not a highlight dump — highlights are the raw material, the note is the processing step.

Keep the source note separate from the ideas you extract. The source note is bibliographic and stays as it is; the ideas graduate into [[Atomic notes]] that no longer mention the source in their title.

Fields worth keeping: author, year, where you found it, and one sentence on *why you picked it up*. That last one is the field you will thank yourself for in two years.
