# Daily notes practice

A daily note is a landing strip, not a destination. Things land there fast and unformatted, and get moved out later — into [[Atomic notes]], tasks, or nothing at all.

The discipline is the moving-out, not the writing-down. A vault of eight hundred daily notes with nothing extracted is a diary, which is fine, but it is not a thinking tool.

Keep the template short. Anything longer than a handful of prompts turns the daily note into paperwork, and paperwork is the thing you stop doing in week three.
