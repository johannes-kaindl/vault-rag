# Notes That Think — reading log

R. Halvorsen, 2019. Picked it up after a year of highlights that never turned into anything.

The central claim is that writing is not what you do *after* thinking — it is the medium thinking happens in, and a note system is therefore a thinking tool rather than an archive.
