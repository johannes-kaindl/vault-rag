# Spaced repetition

Reviewing material at increasing intervals moves it from recognition to recall. The scheduling is the whole trick: review just before you would have forgotten.

For a note system, the useful version is not flashcards but *scheduled re-reading of your own notes*. It surfaces notes you would otherwise never open again, and re-reading your own words tends to produce edits — which is the point.

Note that recall and retrieval are different problems. Spaced repetition fixes what is in your head; [[Semantic search]] fixes what is in your vault.
