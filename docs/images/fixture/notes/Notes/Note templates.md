# Note templates

A template encodes the questions a kind of note should answer. A meeting note that has an empty "Decisions" heading is asking you a question; a blank page is not.

Keep templates to headings and prompts, not prose. The prompts are scaffolding — they should disappear as you fill the note in.

Templates pair well with automated restructuring: if a template describes its sections clearly enough, a messy note can be routed into it without inventing content. What matters is that the original text is *moved*, never rewritten — see [[Retrieval augmented generation]] for why "grounded in your own bytes" is a stronger guarantee than "the model was careful".
