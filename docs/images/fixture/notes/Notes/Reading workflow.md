# Reading workflow

Reading, highlighting, and note-making are three separate activities, and doing them at once does all three badly.

A workable order: read once without a pen. Then go back for the parts that stuck, and highlight those. Then, separately, write [[Literature notes]] from the highlights — in your own words, with the book closed.

The closed book is the important part. Anything you can only write with the source in front of you is a quote, not an understanding.

Related: [[Progressive summarization]], [[Spaced repetition]].
