# Writing habits

Writing daily beats writing well on the days you feel like it. The output of an unreliable writer is not lower quality — it is zero, most weeks.

Two habits carry most of the effect:

- **Stop mid-sentence.** Ending a session at a natural break makes restarting a decision; ending mid-sentence makes it a reflex.
- **Write before input.** Reading first feels like preparation and functions as avoidance.

For note-takers the same rule applies to processing: fifteen minutes of moving things out of [[Daily notes practice]] beats an hour of collecting.
